Structural Integrity Assessment: Eastern Elevation

Prepared by M. Sterling · Oct 24, 2023

Initial surveys of the primary load-bearing structures indicate expected weathering patterns consistent with a facility of this age and exposure. The foundation settling remains within tolerances documented in the original 1998 architectural plans, showing no sign of accelerated degradation in the northern or western quadrants.

However, targeted investigation into the moisture barrier systems has revealed localized failures. Structural assessment of the eastern wall indicates a severe ingress of ambient moisture over the preceding 18 months. This has led to accelerated oxidation of the rebar lattice and subsequent spalling of the concrete facing, particularly concentrated around the secondary drainage vents.

Remediation must be prioritized to prevent further lateral spread of the moisture damage. Immediate sealing of the external micro-fractures, followed by a comprehensive structural reinforced epoxy injection, is recommended. Delaying these interventions could compromise the integrity of the adjacent structural columns, leading to a cascading failure scenario necessitating more invasive and costly repairs.
